Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

NanoClaw is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit authorization, the app accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Contacts) solely to perform tasks the owner requests.

Use and sharing

Data is used only to provide the assistant's functionality to the owner. It is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Google credentials are held by a local credential gateway and are not exposed to the assistant or stored in chat.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/omrih3/nanoclaw-omrih3.
